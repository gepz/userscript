Split the former Flow Chat settings tab in two so neither needs a
scrollbar: Chat Flow holds motion and layout — speed, step timing,
chat limits, the flow conditions, lanes, and the flow area — while a
new Chat Appearance tab holds font, colors, opacity, size, weights,
and the name/emoji display toggles. The flow conditions now sit
nested under the "Flow chats" toggle (renamed from "Create flowing
chats") with a shortened caption, dimming while flow is off; they
stay editable so they can be staged before re-enabling. No setting
changed meaning or default.
