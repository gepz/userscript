Split the Flow Chat settings tab in two so neither needs a scrollbar:
a new Appearance tab holds font, colors, opacity, size, weights, and
the name/emoji display toggles, while Flow Chat keeps motion and
layout — speed, step timing, chat limits, the flow conditions, lanes,
and the flow area. No setting changed meaning or default; they only
moved.
